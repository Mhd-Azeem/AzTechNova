Common questions

Before we begin.

How long will my website take?

We'll agree on a realistic timeline after reviewing your pages, content and requested features.

Is hosting included in the price?

Free-tier hosting and a free subdomain can be set up in your own account, subject to provider terms and limits. Custom domains and any paid hosting are discussed separately.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes, subject to the platform and access available. Send your current link and requirements.

Can I request something not listed?

Yes. Custom projects are quoted individually after a brief discussion.

How do revisions work?

Every website package includes 5 revision rounds within the agreed project scope. Additional revisions or major new features are quoted separately.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Payment stages and any deposit are agreed in writing before the project starts.

Will I own my website?

Ownership, access and handover deliverables are confirmed in the project agreement, including any third-party licenses.

Can you help after launch?

Maintenance and future changes can be discussed as a separate service based on your needs.
